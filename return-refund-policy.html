Return, Refund & Cancellation Policy

Clear, fair, and documented return and refund procedures designed to ensure complete peace of mind with every order.

1. Policy Overview

At VALORA, customer satisfaction and confidence are paramount. We strive to provide accurate imagery, thorough sizing guides, and quality-inspected products. If you are not fully satisfied with your purchase, our transparent return, exchange, and refund process ensures a smooth resolution.

2. Order Cancellation

Before Dispatch: You may cancel an order directly from your My Account → Orders dashboard at any time prior to shipment or by reaching our customer concierge team.

Instant Refund on Cancellation: If your cancelled order was paid online (Full Online or Advance Payment via Razorpay / Cards / UPI), a 100% refund is initiated immediately to your original payment method. The refund reflects in your account within 5 to 7 business days depending on your bank.

After Dispatch: Once an order is handed over to our logistics partner and is in transit, it cannot be cancelled online. In such cases, you may decline the parcel at the time of delivery or initiate a standard return once received.

3. Return Windows & Eligibility

Return windows are calibrated according to product collection and store settings:

  • Main VALORA Lifestyle Catalog: Eligible items may be returned or exchanged within 30 calendar days of confirmed delivery.
  • Sarojini Bazaar Surplus Drops: Due to rapid inventory turnover of surplus and street drops, items carry a 7-Day Hassle-Free Replacement or Return window from delivery date.

Product Condition Requirements:

  • Items must be unworn, unwashed, unaltered, and free of stains, scents, or scuffs.
  • Original tags, designer branding labels, and protective seals must remain attached.
  • Footwear and horology must include their original branded presentation boxes, dust bags, and warranty documentation intact.

4. Damaged, Defective, or Wrong Product Received

If an item arrives damaged in transit, defective in craftsmanship, or differs from the item ordered:

  • Report the issue within 48 hours of delivery via your Account Orders section or by emailing support@velorastudio.com with photographs of the item and packaging.
  • VALORA arranges an immediate doorstep replacement or a 100% full refund with zero pickup or shipping deductions.

5. Open Box Delivery

Where Open Box Delivery is selected or supported by carrier logistics:

  • You may inspect the physical package and outer appearance of the contents in the presence of the delivery courier before paying the final COD balance or providing the delivery OTP.
  • If the product is damaged or incorrect, you may refuse delivery on the spot. If an online advance was paid, it is refunded automatically.

6. Return Pickup Process

Once your return request is logged and approved:

  1. A reverse-pickup schedule is generated with our logistics partner (Delhivery, Ekart, or XpressBees).
  2. The courier will arrive within 24 to 48 hours for doorstep package verification and collection.
  3. A digital pickup acknowledgment receipt with reverse tracking number will be provided upon handover.

7. Refund Timelines & Methods

All refunds are initiated promptly once the returned item arrives at our fulfillment facility and passes quality inspection (usually within 24–48 hours of receipt):

Payment Method Refund Destination Expected Processing Time
Credit / Debit Card (Razorpay) Original Card Source Account 5 – 7 Business Days
Netbanking (Razorpay) Original Bank Account 3 – 5 Business Days
UPI (Google Pay / PhonePe / Paytm / BHIM) Original Linked VPA / Bank 24 – 48 Hours
Cash on Delivery (COD) Customer Verified Bank Account (NEFT / UPI) 3 – 5 Business Days after pickup

8. Advance + COD Refunds

For orders utilizing the hybrid Advance + COD option:

  • If cancelled before delivery: The prepaid advance commitment amount is refunded 100% back to the originating online payment channel.
  • If returned after completed delivery: The advance portion is returned to the original payment channel and the remaining balance paid in cash is transferred via secure NEFT/UPI bank transfer.

9. Non-Returnable Categories

For health, personal hygiene, and safety considerations under standard e-commerce regulations, the following items cannot be accepted for return or exchange unless delivered in a physically damaged or defective state:

  • Fragrances, personal mist sprays, and cosmetics with broken protective foils or packaging seals.
  • Undergarments, innerwear, socks, and intimate apparel.
  • Items expressly designated as "Final Clearance / As-Is".
