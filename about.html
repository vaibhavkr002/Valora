1. Vision & Origins

Founded with the vision to celebrate personal identity through refined essentials, VELORA was conceived in New Delhi to answer a simple question: Why should world-class luxury footwear, timepieces, and accessories be out of reach for thoughtful, style-forward individuals?

From day one, we set out to bypass wasteful intermediaries, curate authentic heritage brands alongside bespoke capsule releases, and deliver a tactile shopping experience grounded in integrity, fast express delivery, and transparent pricing.

Our Manifesto: "Style is not about volume—it is the unspoken confidence of curated quality. Every piece we present is engineered to outlast fleeting seasonal trends."

2. Artisanal Craftsmanship

Every sneaker sole, chronograph dial, leather hide, and cotton thread in our collection undergoes strict quality assurance. We work directly with certified global manufacturing partners and premier authorized distributors.

3. Premium Materials

We never compromise on tactile feel. From breathable combed cotton and heavy French terry fleece to full-grain vegetable-tanned leathers, our materials are selected for how they age gracefully through everyday wear.

4. Authenticity & Service Pledge

Every branded timepiece, shoe, and luxury accessory on VELORA is 100% genuine and verified. We back every order with:

  • Live Courier Tracking: Instant shipment updates via our integrated courier network (BlueDart, Delhivery, Shadowfax, DTDC).
  • 30-Day Easy Returns: Doorstep return pickups and hassle-free replacements if the fit is not ideal.
  • Bank-Grade Security: 256-bit SSL encrypted checkout and reliable Cash on Delivery verification.

5. Conscious Luxury

Our packaging is crafted from 100% recyclable, FSC-certified cardboard, eliminating single-use plastics from customer dispatches. Explore our detailed Sustainability Pledge to learn more about our carbon footprint reduction initiatives.
