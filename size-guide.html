Size & Fit Guide

Detailed measurements and international sizing conversions engineered to ensure your items fit flawlessly.

Finding your ideal fit is essential for comfort and confidence. Consult our comprehensive sizing charts below for footwear, tailored apparel, caps, and luxury horology straps.

VADI footwear is engineered according to true-to-size standard US sizing. If you possess a wider foot profile or fall between two sizes, we recommend selecting the next half-size up.
