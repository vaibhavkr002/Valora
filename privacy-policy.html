1. Introduction & Scope

VADI ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to providing a secure, transparent, and respectful digital shopping experience. This Privacy Policy outlines how information is gathered, utilized, stored, and safeguarded when you explore our digital storefront, browse footwear, timepieces, caps, and apparel, create an account, save wishlist items, or place an order.

By accessing or using the VADI website, you acknowledge the informational and data handling practices described herein. If you do not agree with these principles, we advise discontinuing use of the website.

2. Information We Collect

In the course of operating a modern e-commerce storefront, certain pieces of information are submitted voluntarily by you (such as when filling out a checkout form or creating an account), while other technical parameters are recognized automatically by your web browser during navigation.

3. Account Information

When you register for a VADI member account, we collect your full name, email address, and secure authentication credentials. Account credentials and authentication states are protected and encrypted through our secure backend infrastructure, enabling seamless profile management, order history tracking, and saved preferences.

4. Contact & Support Data

When you submit an inquiry through our Contact Support form, careers application, or affiliate request, we collect your name, email address, optional telephone number, order reference number, and message text. Support tickets are assigned a unique reference code (e.g. #VEL-TKT-XXXXX) for rapid assistance and customer service communication.

5. Order & Transaction Data

When placing an order through Checkout, we collect necessary recipient delivery information (shipping name, delivery address, apartment or suite, city, state, postal code, and contact phone number). Completed orders and tracking records are securely linked to your account and accessible in real-time in your My Account → Orders dashboard.

6. Payment Information

Zero Raw Card Data Stored: Payment processing is handled through industry-standard tokenization protocols and certified payment gateways. VADI never stores raw credit card numbers, CVVs, or full payment credentials on its servers.

7. Device & Usage Information

When accessing VADI, standard non-identifying technical information is processed by your browser to ensure optimal visual rendering. This includes screen resolution, viewport width, browser type, operating system, and language preferences. This information enables our responsive design system to serve mobile drawers, touch carousels, or multi-column grids appropriately.

8. Cookies & Local Storage

VADI utilizes modern web browser localStorage rather than intrusive third-party tracking cookies. The keys utilized across our site include:

  • velora_cart: Retains your active shopping bag items, chosen sizes, colors, and quantities.
  • velora_wishlist: Stores your bookmarked products for convenient access.
  • velora_user_session: Maintains your authenticated member state while navigating between pages.
  • velora_remembered_email: Pre-fills your login email if "Remember Me" is checked.

9. How We Use Information

Information collected within VADI is used strictly to fulfill core e-commerce interactions:

  • Processing and fulfilling your orders, invoices, receipts, and live tracking timelines.
  • Maintaining persistent cart item counts and wishlist badges in the header navigation.
  • Filtering and sorting catalog items according to category parameters and deals queries.
  • Providing responsive customer support ticket confirmations and processing inquiries.

10. How We Protect Information

We employ structured client-side form validation, sanitized text rendering, and secure coding practices to prevent cross-site scripting (XSS) and injection errors. All transactions and data transfers are protected with end-to-end SSL/TLS encryption and strict access controls.

11. How Information May Be Shared

We do not sell your data: VADI does not monetize, license, or sell user email addresses, browsing habits, or order records to external telemarketers, ad networks, or data brokers.

12. Third-Party Services

Our website may reference links to external platforms such as social media channels or carrier websites. These external sites operate under independent privacy protocols. We encourage customers to inspect external privacy terms before providing information on third-party sites.

13. Analytics & Product Metrics

Product popularity metrics, review counts, and badges displayed across our catalog are calculated from store activity without deploying invasive third-party telemetry or cross-site tracking.

14. Data Retention

Information stored in your web browser's local cache remains active until cleared through your browser settings or explicitly removed through the "Sign Out" or "Reset Filters" controls on VADI.

15. User Choices & Rights

You maintain total control over your browsing session data:

  • Access & Review: View all saved order histories and shipping addresses inside your My Account panel.
  • Cart & Wishlist Clear: Remove individual saved items or clear the entire bag using the in-drawer controls.
  • Sign Out: Terminate your active session securely with one click in the header account menu.

16. Account Deletion & Data Privacy Rights

To request permanent deletion of your account or data associated with your profile, contact our support team at support@vadistudio.com or manage your saved preferences directly within My Account.

17. Children’s Privacy

VADI is designed for adult fashion enthusiasts, horology collectors, and general consumers aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from individuals under the age of 16.

18. Policy Updates & Contact Us

We may periodically revise this Privacy Policy to reflect enhancements in site features, legal updates, or backend API migrations (such as Supabase or Stripe connectivity). Changes will be documented on this page alongside an updated date.

For questions regarding our privacy principles, connect with our team through the Contact Support page or email support@vadistudio.com.

Have Questions About Our Policies?

Our customer care concierge is available to clarify any terms, data practices, order guidelines, or return inquiries.
