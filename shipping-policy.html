Order Processing Times

All orders placed on VADI are processed at our central fulfillment centers Monday through Friday, excluding national holidays. Orders placed before 2:00 PM EST are typically prepared and dispatched on the same business day. Orders placed after the 2:00 PM cutoff, or on weekends, begin processing the next business day.

Customized or limited-edition horology timepieces and tailored outerwear may require an additional 24-48 hours for delicate inspection, precision calibration, and protective luxury packaging.

Free Shipping Eligibility

VADI offers 100% Free Express Delivery on all orders across India with absolutely no minimum order value requirement. Every product, brand, category, deal, and BOGO order ships free with zero shipping fee at checkout.

Unforeseen Delivery Delays

While 98% of shipments arrive within estimated transit windows, occasional delays can occur due to severe weather events, carrier route congestion during holiday periods, or customs border inspections. If your shipment has exceeded its projected delivery date by more than 3 business days, please connect with our support team immediately.

Incorrect Address & Address Changes

Please verify your shipping address carefully before submitting checkout. Because our automated warehouse systems initiate order packing within minutes, address changes cannot be guaranteed once an order is confirmed. If you notice an error, contact us immediately with your order number.

International Shipping & Customs

VADI proudly ships to over 45 international destinations via DHL Express International. International transit typically takes 5 to 9 business days. Applicable import taxes, customs tariffs, and local VAT are calculated during checkout where supported, or collected by the carrier upon customs clearance.

Damaged or Lost Packages

Every package is insured during transit. If your package arrives with visible exterior damage or contents missing, take photos of the box and contact our concierge within 48 hours of delivery. We will open an investigation and immediately issue a replacement or full refund.

Need Assistance with a Delivery?

Our customer care concierge is available 7 days a week to help locate shipments, clarify delivery times, or handle carrier claims.
