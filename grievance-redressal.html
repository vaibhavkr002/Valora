1. Statutory Framework

VALORA is committed to operating an ethical, lawful, and consumer-centric digital commerce platform. Pursuant to the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020, and the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021, this portal provides a structured escalation path for consumers whose concerns have not been resolved through regular customer care channels.

3. How to File a Formal Grievance

To enable prompt investigation and audit of your concern, please provide the following information when lodging your grievance:

  • Order Identification: Your full order reference number (e.g. #VEL-XXXXX).
  • Registered Credentials: The customer full name, email address, and mobile number registered with the purchase.
  • Previous Ticket Reference: Any previous correspondence ID with customer care.
  • Factual Summary: A concise statement describing the unresolved issue (e.g., undelivered package, refund delay, damaged product dispute).
  • Supporting Documentation: Photographs of product packaging, courier receipt, or invoice copy.

4. Statutory Timelines & Progress Tracking

VALORA strictly adheres to the mandated statutory timelines:

  • Acknowledgment: Every grievance receives an official acknowledgment and reference code within 48 hours of receipt.
  • Final Redressal: The designated grievance desk investigates and communicates the formal resolution or corrective action within 15 working days from the date of receipt.
